Unity C# Port

Pocket Mirror ~ GoldenerTraum (port)

Pocket Mirror ~ GoldenerTraum is a story-driven horror RPG that follows a young girl trapped within a mysterious world shaped by memories, dreams and hidden truths. Through exploration, puzzles and narrative-driven gameplay, players uncover the secrets behind its surreal environments and the characters they encounter.


I joined the development roughly three months after the project began, when the conversion tool was already close to completion. The tool converted RPG Maker JSON data into Visual Scripting graphs using custom C# nodes developed specifically for the port. My contribution focused on implementing and refining these custom nodes to support the remaining game content. After conversion, I tested each room against the original game, fixing issues either through custom code or by adjusting the generated graphs.

About Me

I got into game development during my final year of vocational school, when I created a survival game in Unity as my year-long final project. Combined with my passion for video games since childhood, this project made me realize how much I enjoy bringing ideas to life and creating something that other people can experience and enjoy.

Since then, I’ve continued to grow as a programmer, working with different technologies, game engines, and projects.

What I enjoy most about game development is creating playable, interactive experiences. I’m particularly interested in gameplay, mechanics, player interactions, and seeing how different systems come together to shape the experience.

I enjoy finding clean and effective solutions, seeing my work tested, and improving it through feedback. I work with Unity and Unreal Engine and have also contributed to ports across different platforms.
